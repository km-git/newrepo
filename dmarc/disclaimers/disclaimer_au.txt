This Email Deliverability & Brand-Protection Review is observational only.

It records DNS, SPF, DKIM, DMARC aggregate (RUA), forensic (RUF), and heuristic
inbox-placement findings for the named domain at the time of the run. It is not
legal advice, not an audit opinion, not a warranty of inbox placement, and not a
substitute for an email-security, anti-phishing, or anti-spam control operated
by a partner MSP.

The operator does not run anti-spam gateways, sandbox detonation, or URL rewriting.
Active filtering work is referred to partner MSPs. Do not treat pass rates,
key lengths, or placement labels as a contractual or regulatory outcome.

No representation is made that mail will arrive, that spoofing will stop, or that
a DMARC policy change is appropriate. Premature `p=reject` can drop legitimate
mail. Policy changes remain the customer's decision.

Generated by the free OSS mosaic (parsedmarc / dnspython / DuckDB / Plotly /
Presidio when installed). $0/month tooling. Vendor-neutral: this review does not
recommend commercial DMARC vendors.
